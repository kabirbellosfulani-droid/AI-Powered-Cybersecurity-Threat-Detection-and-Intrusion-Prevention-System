Rules14 transparent indicators Examplesbrute force, SYN flood, scan, exfil Purposehuman-readable evidence
Mirrors how Suricata/Snort rules work: thresholds on raw fields. Two supporting rules (or one strong rule) can raise an alert even when the ML models are not confident — exactly the "defence in depth" idea.
